The Concept

The Veilence is a concept inspired by the meaning of “Shape of My Heart,” exploring the journey of looking back, facing our mistakes, and revealing the parts of ourselves we once chose to hide. The “veil” represents the layers we create to conceal our regrets, emotions, flaws, and the versions of ourselves we were afraid to show. Like the feeling of having played a part and kept the truth in the dark, we sometimes become someone we think we are supposed to be, while slowly losing sight of who we truly are.

Yet change begins when we have the courage to lift that veil and face what lies beneath it. The Veilence is not about erasing the past or becoming someone completely new. It is about accepting every mistake, understanding every regret, and acknowledging the experiences that shaped us.

Because perhaps knowing ourselves means looking back at who we were without turning away from what we see. We are not defined by the things we wish we could change, but by our willingness to face them, learn from them, and move forward.

And when there is nothing left to hide, what remains is the shape of our heart—the truth of who we have always been beneath it all.

General Rules

  1. We welcome Gen!RP and Selective accounts. One head per account; side accounts are allowed as long as they remain active alongside the main. Username, display name, and bio must stay relevant to your muse.
  2. A maximum of 3 OAs, including TheVeilence, and 2 SQs is allowed. Group DMs do not count. Keep your following under 500; new accounts should have at least 250 tweets and 50 followings.
  3. Manual updates and official retweets are both accepted. Manual updates are highly encouraged as part of keeping your muse active. Official retweets are accepted, but personal updates are always preferred.
  4. Interact with at least 7 Wanderers daily and avoid CIC habits. We encourage a wide circle and active participation throughout the OA.
  5. JFB, nickname/upfoll schemes, bots, and drama-seeking are not allowed. Keep RL matters inside brackets and refrain from interacting with non-RP content.
  6. NSFW content of any kind is strictly prohibited. Respect your fellow Wanderers, Luminars, and other spaces. Keep the environment comfortable for everyone.
  7. Participation in missions and activities is required. If real life gets busy, let us know beforehand.
  8. Upon verification, follow our main base, sub-base, and fellow OAs. Keep your presence here genuine, respectful, and within the spirit of the space.

Activity & Status

  1. Semi-hiatus is limited to 3 days, while hiatus is limited to 5 days. Longer breaks will not be accommodated.
  2. Temporary character swaps are allowed once during the OA period, excluding swaps made for other OA missions. Account moves are permitted twice, with exceptions only for suspended or locked accounts.
  3. Keep us informed of any changes to your username, character, or account. Username/account moves should be updated within 1 week after verification, while character changes should be made within 3 days.
// 05

Warnings & Unverification

  1. Drama, CIC, war, or deliberate rule-breaking may result in immediate unverification.
  2. Inactivity for 24 hours after verification or after a hiatus ends, as well as 72 hours of inactivity without notice, will result in unverification. Deactivation without notice must be reported within 24 hours to avoid the same consequence.
  3. Missing two activities or missions without notice will result in a warning.
  4. Three warnings will lead to unverification.
